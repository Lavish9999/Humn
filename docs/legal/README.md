# Legal document placeholders — attorney review required

Before launch, counsel must review and approve: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Human-Created Content Policy, Community Guidelines, Copyright/DMCA Policy, Appeal Policy, Creator Commerce Terms, and Subscription Terms.

The product must not claim perfect AI detection or “100% proven human.” Policies must distinguish ordinary editing from substantive generative creation, explain evidence handling, document appeal rights, and disclose retention/deletion practices.
